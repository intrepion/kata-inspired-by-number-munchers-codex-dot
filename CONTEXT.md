# Lumen Orchard
A mathematical garden where a lantern keeper harvests matching seeds while avoiding restless thorns.

## Language
**Harvest**: An explicit attempt to eat the seed in the keeper's current cell.
_Avoid_: Click answer, automatic collection

**Rule**: The mathematical condition that identifies harvestable seeds.

**Patch**: One fixed six-column, five-row grid of seeds.

**Shelter**: A temporary marked cell that excludes thorns.

**Thorn**: A roaming hazard. A Drifter travels straight; a Tracker pursues the keeper.

**Recovery**: A stopped interval after a mistake or collision, followed by a brief protective glow.
